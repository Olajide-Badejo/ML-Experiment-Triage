Spearman rank correlation, two sided, with the p value taken from permuting the pairings rather than from a t approximation that is invalid under ties. The unit is the condition, not the run: seed replicates of one setting are averaged onto one point first, because eight seeds of one learning rate are eight measurements of one point and not eight draws of the parameter. The n variants column is therefore not decoration: a correlation of 0.9 over four conditions is not evidence of anything, and below five conditions no p value is reported at all because none could be small enough to mean anything. p adj is Benjamini Hochberg across this whole table, which is one family of questions asked at once. These are associations across the sweep as it was actually run, not controlled effects, so a parameter that only ever changed alongside another cannot be separated from it here.

Strong mode
Seed replicated permutation test. Null hypothesis: the condition label is exchangeable across runs. Seed to seed variance is inside the null distribution.
Weak mode
Single run window block permutation test, block length three times the estimated autocorrelation time. Null hypothesis: the two final windows are blockwise exchangeable. This mode cannot see seed to seed variance and is labelled weaker wherever it appears.
